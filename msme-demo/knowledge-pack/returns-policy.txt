Returns & damage
Version: 2026-11-20
Public company knowledge — fictional demo business.

Report damaged, defective or incorrect items within 7 calendar days of delivery, with the order number, photographs and affected quantities. Keep the goods and original packaging until review is complete. Approved claims may receive replacement items or a credit note after inspection. Custom-printed packaging is not returnable for a change of mind; verified manufacturing defects remain eligible for review. Used items are not eligible. Returns require prior authorization. The chatbot can explain this policy but cannot approve refunds, issue credits or cancel an order.
