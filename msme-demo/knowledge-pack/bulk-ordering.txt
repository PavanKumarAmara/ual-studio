Bulk orders & minimums
Version: 2026-11-20
Public company knowledge — fictional demo business.

Standard minimum quantities depend on the product and are shown in the catalogue: most shipping boxes and mailers start at 100 units; heavy-duty dispatch boxes start at 50; takeaway bowls and lunch boxes start at 200; stand-up pouches start at 250; packing tape starts at 24 rolls; paper wrap starts at 6 rolls. Custom printing has a minimum order of 500 units per design and size. Request a quote for orders of 1,000 units or more; no fixed volume discount is promised. Custom artwork must be approved before production. This demo does not submit real quote requests or create orders.
