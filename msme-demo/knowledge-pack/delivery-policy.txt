Delivery policy
Version: 2026-11-20
Public company knowledge — fictional demo business.

Standard in-stock orders are dispatched within 2–3 business days after order confirmation. Typical delivery after dispatch is 2–4 business days within Karnataka and 4–7 business days elsewhere in India. Custom-printed orders require 10–15 business days after artwork approval. These windows are estimates, not guaranteed arrival dates. Sundays and public holidays are excluded. Shipping charges are confirmed with an order, not included in catalogue prices. If an order is delayed, check its current shipment timeline and contact support with the order number. Never infer an individual order's status from this policy; the order database is the source of truth.
